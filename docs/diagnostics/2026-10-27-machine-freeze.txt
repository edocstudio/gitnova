Local verification interruption — 2026-10-27

Development verification was paused following workstation freezes and delayed
executable launches. Observations were collected after restart, so they do not
establish the root cause or the state of GitNova during the interruption.

Public engineering outcome:
- Do not overlap build/test/native-launch runners.
- Use bounded build concurrency and focused integration targets.
- Keep at most one task-owned diagnostic launch probe; do not start repeated
  copies of stalled executables.
- Collect read-only system evidence before resuming verification.
- A successful later build does not prove the original freeze was fixed.

Raw workstation process inventory, security-product details and account-specific
paths are intentionally excluded from this public record.
